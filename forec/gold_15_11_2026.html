2. Why (Fundamental & Microstructure Breakdown)

2.1 Fundamental Drivers & Macro Primacy: Rates and the dollar drive gold now. CME FedWatch puts a 25 bp hike on Dec 9 at 91% and 50 bp at 9%, after an October PPI slightly cooler than expected: +0.3% m/m vs 0.4% forecast, 6.5% y/y vs 6.6% (TokenPost, Nov 13). The fed funds range is 3.75–4.00%. The 10-year real yield climbed to 2.86% (+8.7% in five days), the 10-year nominal to 5.10%, and the US Dollar Index hit a 19-month high above 103. The hike is already priced; a fresh leg down needs talk of 50 bp. Demand is soft: GLD lost $1.53 billion over 30 days, India's October gold imports fell 62.57%, and jewellery demand declined for a 13th straight quarter. The floor: global gold ETFs still added 19 tonnes in October to a record 4,275 tonnes, though North America shed 4.5 tonnes (Money Metals / WGC, Nov 6), and Goldman expects central banks to buy about 60 tonnes a month. No new tariff decisions on gold bullion were found in the last seven days.
2.2 OI, COT, Option Walls & Curve:
  • • Speculators Have Already Left: managed money is net long 104,297 contracts (as of Nov 3), down for ten straight weeks and at the 38th annual percentile. Small traders sit at the 2nd percentile, and the gap between speculators and hedgers is at the 13th. This removes the fuel for a deep liquidation.
  • • Hedgers Buy the Dip: producers cut their net short by 5,591 contracts to -7,990 (98th percentile, the least short of the year), and swap dealers cut theirs by 26,149. A delayed COT report on Nov 16 shows whether this continued.
  • • Option Walls (OGZ26, expiry Nov 24, as of Nov 11): the put wall at 3,900 (6,462) sits right at price, and the put cluster at 3,700 totals 18,638 contracts. Call walls are at 4,100 (5,484, calc.) and 4,200 (14,580, calc.), then 4,500 (11,263), 4,725 (9,534) and 4,800 (8,659). The put/call ratio rose to 0.85 from 0.78 as positioning shifts to puts. Options stop trading four business days before month-end (CME rule via deltavalue.de), so the walls matter most this week and next.
  • • Futures Curve: mild contango of +0.2% to the next contract and +1.0% to February (GCG27): no physical tightness. COMEX registered stocks hit a new low of 14.22 million oz while total stocks fell to 24.58 million oz.
2.3 Cross-Asset & Relative Value: The whole precious metals complex is under pressure: silver fell 5.4% and platinum 3.1% over five days. Over 21 days gold is down 3.0% against 3.7% for the Nasdaq-100, so this is a rates-and-dollar move, not flight from risk. Middle East tension now works against gold: it lifts oil and inflation expectations, which adds to the case for Fed hikes.

RSI(14) = 35.9, and price is -7.1% below the 50-day SMA (8th percentile). MFI runs 8.1 points above RSI: money is coming in ahead of price. The trend is still down: the 20-day SMA has been below the 50-day for 32 sessions, and price is below the lower edge of the 3-month volume bank at $4,047.80. ATR(14) is 2.36%, about $92 a day, so zone A is two to three sessions of follow-through away.
